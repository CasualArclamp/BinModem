# U.S. Robotics x2

[Back to the README](../../README.md)

x2 is the 56k scheme that U.S. Robotics shipped in 1997, before V.90.

## What the server offers

A BinModem server also offers x2 when you set `ATS70=1` (or the **x2** tick of
the window) with `AT+MS=V90` or `V92`.

Some callers offer V.34 and no PCM in V.8, as a USR modem without its V.90
upgrade does. Such a caller then gets x2: PCM down at 32000 to 56000, and V.34
up. All other callers get what they got before.

## Sources

No part of x2 has a standard. The facts come from the notes of libv90modem.
Those notes came from D-Modem, from courier-emu and from its own study of the
server firmware. The level banks for each rate are data from the published
numbers of D-Modem. See
[crates/datapump/src/x2](../../crates/datapump/src/x2).

## Test with the Courier firmware

The Courier firmware of U.S. Robotics connects to the server when the firmware
runs in courier-emu:

- The terminal of the Courier shows `CONNECT 49333/ARQ/x2/LAPM/V42BIS`.
- Numbered lines arrive intact.
- BinModem follows the rate change of the Courier to 54666.
- The data continues to arrive after the rate change.

This work found two facts:

- A Courier reads the V.8 JM of the server for a carrier inversion between one
  JM and the next. It reads on the bit boundary where the next JM starts, and
  not one bit earlier.
- V.42 in BinModem answered an XID with its own offer, not with the settled
  values. A Courier reads that as no answer. This is fixed for every call, not
  only for x2.

[tools/x2/courier-peer.md](../../tools/x2/courier-peer.md) tells you how to
run the emulated Courier.

BinModem has not met a real x2 modem yet.
